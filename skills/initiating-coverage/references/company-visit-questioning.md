# 公司调研提问策略 (Company Visit Questioning Framework)

When researching companies for equity reports, direct questions about undisclosed strategic plans backfire — management legally cannot selectively disclose material non-public information, and asking directly exposes that you're speculating rather than verifying.

This framework provides tactical question designs that extract signals without triggering regulatory or diplomatic barriers.

## Core Principle

**Ask about capability boundaries, not strategic intent.** The goal is to make management reveal whether they've *evaluated* a direction — not whether they've *decided* to pursue it.

## Question Archetypes

### 1. Technical Capability Probe (技术通用性试探)

Ask about technical adaptability, not strategic roadmap. Signals to watch: whether management can fluently discuss the target domain's technical parameters without pausing to think.

Example: "咱们的喷头波形调校技术，如果切换到高粘度UV固化油墨体系，适配难度大概有多大？研发周期有评估过吗？"

If they answer with specific parameters and timelines → they've evaluated it. If they say "这个方向我们没碰过" → they haven't.

### 2. Customer Overlap Probe (客户重叠度试探)

Ask whether customers in the target domain have reached out. This is safe because it's about *inbound* interest, not *outbound* strategy.

Example: "PCB行业里比如鹏鼎控股、深南电路这类客户，他们在数码化转型上有没有跟咱们接触过？"

"有接触过" or "他们确实在找替代方案" → demand side is already engaging.

### 3. Competitor Benchmarking Probe (竞争对手对标试探)

Frame as a hypothetical comparison with a known competitor in the target space. Management's willingness and fluency in discussing the competitor reveals whether they've studied the space.

Example: "打一个比方，如果咱们把喷墨打印的核心能力平移到PCB阻焊环节，跟江苏汉印相比，咱们的优劣势会在哪里？"

This is not asking "are you entering" — it's asking "how would you compare if you did." Management who engages enthusiastically with this hypothetical is signaling interest.

### 4. R&D Pipeline Probe (研发管线试探)

Ask broadly about non-core R&D directions, not specifically about the target domain. Wide-net approach gives them room to hint.

Example: "公司在研发费用里有没有布局喷头以外的泛工业应用方向？比如非纺织领域的喷墨技术预研？"

"我们在看几个方向，有些已经有demo了" → biggest possible signal without a formal announcement.

## Signal Hierarchy

When piecing together management responses, rank the signals:

| Signal Strength | What They Say |
|----------------|---------------|
| 🔴 Strong | Knows target domain technical parameters, can name competitors' product lines, knows end-customer pain points, admits to pre-research |
| 🟡 Moderate | Engages with hypotheticals, shows domain awareness but avoids specifics, references "industry trends" in the target space |
| 🟢 Weak | "目前聚焦主业", "暂时没有研究那个方向", avoids the topic entirely |

## Pitfalls

- Never ask "are you planning to enter X market?" — this forces either a lie or a regulatory violation
- Don't ask chain questions that build on each other — keep each probe independent so management can dodge one without derailing all
- If management volunteers information unprompted, note it but don't push — they're already giving you what they can legally share
- The gap between "we've evaluated it" and "we're committed" is real — don't over-read moderate signals as confirmation
