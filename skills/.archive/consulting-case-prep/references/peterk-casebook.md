## Extraction notes

- Each case follows a standard structure: a title page → `Prompt` + `Additional information` (interviewer-only facts, "provide only upon request") + `Comments` (candidate expectations, expected time) → a "Case questions" list → per-question answer pages.
- Answer pages carry a recurring note: "This is just one of many potential ways to structure/ brainstorm… treat as a reference point and develop your own style." Condense — don't copy that boilerplate.
- **Brainstorming questions** expect "at least 4 ideas, best practice 8+"; the answer is a structured bullet list under 2–4 buckets.
- **Math questions** have a prompt page, a "calculations" page (the step-by-step), and a "contextualization" page split into *Basic comments* (expected from everyone) and *Advanced comments* (for outstanding candidates). Keep both tiers in the condensed official answer, but tag them.
- **Chart questions** reference an "Appendix 1 / Hand-outs" page with the actual chart data; the prompt page says "[Please provide Appendix 1 to the candidate]".
- Cases #4 (European Beauty Company) and #5–8 (Almond farm, Banknote manufacturer, Fast casual food restaurant, Instant coffee pod producer) are already fully written to Notion — skip them when batch-extracting. Firm on the title page is authoritative (cases #5/6/7/8 are McKinsey/Bain/Bain/Bain respectively).
